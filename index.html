Texture technology

Ultra-Fit Bubble System™

Three things happen in the two seconds after you pump: the double-mesh pump whips the liquid into micro-foam, the foam bursts against your skin, and the actives ride the water layer into the surface of the stratum corneum.

Step 01

Micro bubble

Two mesh layers turn the essence into ultra-fine foam that hugs the skin without running.

Step 02

Bubble burst

The foam breaks on contact. That tiny pressure change opens the absorption channel — no rubbing.

Step 03

Epidermal ultra-fit

Actives travel in the water layer and settle into the surface layers of the stratum corneum.

Compliance corner

Say this. Not that.

TikTok Shop reviewers are strict about skincare claims. These swaps keep the content punchy without promising a medical outcome.

✓ Safe to say

  • “Helps balance oil and water so makeup sits better.”
  • “Helps soothe the look of redness after one use.”
  • “Pores look less visible after two weeks.”
  • “Preps skin for smoother-looking foundation.”
  • “Clinically tested for low skin irritation.”
  • “Feels weightless and non-sticky under makeup.”

✕ Avoid

  • “Treats or fights acne / antibacterial.”
  • “Pores disappear instantly.”
  • “Zero irritation / fragrance-free.”
  • “Guarantees no caking or pilling.”
  • “Replaces all your skincare.”
  • “Invented before/after proof.”
